Who We Are

We are a Presbyterian church in Singapore, a Christ-centered community committed to worship, discipleship, and mission.

We affirm the historic Christian faith expressed in the Apostles’ Creed and Reformed tradition.

Member church of The Presbyterian Church in Singapore
